## 7. Why Decisions Were Changed
- **Unit Economics Rigor:** AI lacks innate fiscal discipline unless constrained. When forced to calculate `CAC = Budget / Signups`, the model readily admitted that ₹2,000 cannot sustain paid acquisition for 500 leads.
- **Friction vs. Conversion Trade-offs:** AI initially suggested requiring students to write a detailed ML project architecture before attending. Human judgment recognized this created immense top-of-funnel friction. We re-framed it as an **Optional AI Project Idea** with just 3 inputs, reassuring students that ready-to-run templates are provided on Day 7.
- **Attendance Defense:** AI treated registration as the terminal finish line. In real-world webinar operations, 60–70% of free registrants ghost on event day. Human judgment introduced the **4-Tier Attendance Defense Engine** (T-24h to Post-Session) to guarantee 220+ live attendees.

---

## 8. Human Judgment & Strategic Synthesis
The defining factor of this project is that **AI operated as a junior engineer, while human judgment served as the Lead Product Architect and Growth Lead**:
1. **Strategic Reframing:** Instead of viewing the challenge as *"How do I design a web page?"*, human judgment framed it as *"How do I design a viral loop where every registration produces >0.6 new registrations?"*.
2. **Behavioral Psychology:** Leveraging inter-college rivalry (RVCE vs. CBIT vs. VIT) and creating a live "Rank Takeover Calculator" stems from an understanding of Indian campus culture—something AI does not understand intuitively.
3. **Fiscal Guardrails:** Enforcing a live, itemized ₹2,000 ledger ensures the project remains defensible under brutal evaluator questioning.

## 9. Technical Learnings
- **Next.js 14 App Router Dynamic Resolution:** Implementing real-time query parameter resolution (`/register?ref=CODE`) requiring `<Suspense>` boundary wrapping to support static page generation while maintaining dynamic client-side hydration.
- **Client-Server State Synchronization:** Building a unified `RoleContext` that broadcasts live registration increments to the Navbar target pill, Admin War Room, and recent activity feeds without requiring manual page refreshes.
- **Zero-Dependency Heuristic Modeling:** Implementing deterministic, realistic simulation engines in TypeScript that calculate viral growth, channel attribution, and quality scoring without external server latency.

## 10. Growth-Engineering Learnings
- **The Power of K-Factor Compounding:** When viral multiplier \(K = 0.62\), 216 initial seed registrations generate 134 first-generation referrals, 83 second-generation referrals, 51 third-generation referrals, totaling **530+ students** at ₹0 marginal media cost.
- **Downside Defense & Fallback Engineering (K = 0.30):** Hope is not an operating strategy. If viral referral participation drops to 25% and conversion drops to 34.3% (\(K = 0.30\)), organic yield stalls at 309 signups (191-student shortfall). We engineered an automated Day-3 pacing guardrail backed by our ₹850 unallocated contingency buffer: it automatically activates 17 Emergency Sub-Ambassador Bounties (₹50 each) across high-density hostels, adding +195 direct registrations to deliver **504 total students (100.8%)** at an audited **₹3.97 CAC** within the ₹2,000 cap.
- **Non-Monetary Incentives Beat Cash Discounts:** Cash prizes on micro-budgets attract bounty hunters and spammers. High-perceived-value career assets (ATS Resume Blueprints, Colab GPU passes, 1:1 mentor critiques) attract serious learners who actually show up.
- **Attendance is the True North Star:** A growth marketer who stops at registration has failed. Growth engineering must own the entire retention funnel through D7 live execution.

## 11. Known Prototype Limitations
- **Simulated Messaging Layer:** In this local prototype, WhatsApp sharing utilizes official URL scheme deep links (`https://api.whatsapp.com/send?text=...`) rather than an automated Meta Cloud Business API. Live enterprise deployment would attach a verified Meta WhatsApp Business Account for automated 2-way bot messaging.
- **In-Memory Store Persistence:** State persists in memory and browser `localStorage`. A production multi-tenant deployment would back this with Supabase or PostgreSQL with row-level security.
- **Simulated Campus Data:** College ranks and referral counts represent a realistic simulation model built to test pacing logic, not real-world NxtWave historical campaign metrics.
